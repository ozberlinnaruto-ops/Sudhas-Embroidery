Our Story

Passion Woven into Every Stitch

Sudha's Embroidery Academy was founded with one simple dream — to make the beautiful, intricate arts of Aari and Zardoshi embroidery accessible to every woman in India who wants to learn.

What started as small local workshops grew into a full academy with hundreds of students across India. Our founder Sudha has over 8 years of hands-on experience in hand embroidery and brings warmth, patience, and deep expertise to every class.

We teach Aari and Zardoshi embroidery, brooch making, jewellery crafting, and tracing — giving students both the creative skills and practical knowledge to turn their passion into a profession.
