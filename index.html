About Us

Sudha's Embroidery Academy

We are a passionate embroidery academy dedicated to preserving the timeless arts of Aari and Zardoshi — two of India's most beautiful textile traditions.

With over 8 years of experience, we have trained 500+ students across India in hand embroidery, jewellery making, brooches crafting, and digital design.

Beginner-friendly courses starting from ₹399
Live interactive + self-paced recorded classes
Delivered through ClassPlus with mobile app access
WhatsApp community and direct instructor support
